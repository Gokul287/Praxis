# Ticket #4815 — CDN edge cert expiry (renewal automation regression)

**Status**: closed (resolved)
**Severity**: P0
**Opened**: 2026-04-19 11:48 UTC
**Closed**: 2026-04-19 12:31 UTC

## Summary

ACME-driven cert renewal job stalled in `queued` state for 11 minutes
after a quota change in the staging account. The previous cert chain
expired in production while renewal automation reported no errors,
just zero progress. TLS handshake failure ratio peaked at 41% across
two edge POPs (eu-west-3, us-east-1) before resolution.

## Resolution

1. Issued `rollback_deploy service=cdn` to revert to the previous edge
   build with the still-valid cert chain.
2. Manually triggered cert renewal in the management API (bypassing
   the wedged queue).
3. TLS handshake failure ratio fell to 0.7% within 60 seconds of fleet
   propagation.

## Lessons learned

- ACME renewal automation needs a **liveness probe**, not just a status
  field. Quota-induced stalls present as silent successes.
- Rolling forward (deploying a new cert) is slower than rolling back
  to the prior good revision; prefer rollback during P0 cert incidents.
